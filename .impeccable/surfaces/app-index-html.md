Scope: English League board app (all screens, app/index.html). Mode: Operate. Audience: teacher driving a 1920×1080 touch board; A1–A2 student athletes reading from ≤5 m in a daylit classroom.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The league is the team's locker room: every team owns a painted steel door, every score is stencil paint, every student a strip of tape with their name. Refuses the category default of a dark LED scoreboard with one neon accent.
OWN-WORLD: Graphite steel ground (#1b1f24), locker doors as flat saturated team-colour fields (red/blue/green/orange), stencil numerals (Big Shoulders Stencil Display, tabular) for every score and rank, Big Shoulders Display for words, Barlow Semi Condensed for teacher UI, off-white tape labels with dark ink for names, safety-yellow floor-line paint as the single action colour. Raise from pop sleeve: each game phase floods one flat colour field (move = safety yellow, speak = steel + photo, exit = tape paper). Raise from dive computer: one digit face for all numbers.
STORY: Teacher sees their class lockers, taps one, lands on the panel, runs a game; students watch their door's number get stamped and their tape name climb.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Class select = a row of tall locker doors, one per section, huge stencil "11" + section letter, tape label with team/student counts; last door blank with "+ Şube ekle". Topbar thin steel strip: class badge left, undo + fullscreen right.
FORM: Locker room (my grounded list #7 of 7), seed a4bef279. Signature interaction: point award stamps the stencil number (scale-in 150 ms) and the row slides to its new rank.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
